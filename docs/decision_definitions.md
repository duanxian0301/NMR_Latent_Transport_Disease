# Frozen decision definitions

## Retained factors

The principal set is F9, F11, F13, F14, F15, F17, F18, F20, F22, F23, F24, F25 and F26. The remaining factors retain their original intercept-only, watch or drop classifications. This archive does not retune those decisions.

## Loading concentration

A near-single-trait direction was defined by maximum absolute column-normalized loading ≥0.95. This is a concentration rule, not a test of biological independence.

## Fixed projection versus independent structure

- **Fixed-loading projection:** UK Biobank loadings are held constant while resource-specific GLS weights are used.
- **Independent structure:** GFA is fitted separately in Karjalainen and loading spaces are compared afterward.

These analyses answer different questions and should not be described as equivalent replication estimands.

## Single-metabolite baseline

Factor and dominant-metabolite cross-resource genetic correlations are shown descriptively. Shared GWAS inputs make the two estimates correlated; the archive contains no valid independent difference test between them.

## Locus terminology

“Multivariate-threshold candidate” means that a factor association crossed the prespecified threshold while the constituent-metabolite comparison did not under the audit rule. It does not establish a new, LD-independent locus or a causal mechanism.

## Factor signs

Factor orientation is arbitrary. Reversing all loadings and factor effects leaves the latent direction unchanged. Sign differences alone are not a new biological mechanism.
